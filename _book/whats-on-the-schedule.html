3.3 Cluster meetings

MMBD holds cluster-wide meetings at 4pm on the first Friday of every month. These are predominantly meant for PIs in the cluster, but other cluster members are welcome to join.

As well as administrative matters, these meetings are an opportunity for cluster members to pitch new grant or paper ideas to a reasonably generous audience. If you are interested in putting together a fellowship application (see chapter X) this is a good forum to get feedback on your idea at an early stage of development.

3.4 Social stuff

EEB holds coffee and news mornings every Tuesday at 10:30-11:00 am. MMBD PIs typically migrate to the university pub (the Uni Arms) after cluster meetings on Fridays, while EEB members tend to start gathering there at about 5pm every week.
