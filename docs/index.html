Welcome!

Welcome to the Physics of Microbial Ecosystems (PoME) group! This handbook is intended as your complete guide to life in your new position. If you find it is not a complete guide, please email Oli at o.meacock [at] sheffield.ac.uk with suggestions for additions.

Assuming you have access to the School of Biosciences’ intranet, a lot of useful information can be found here. If you’ve not made it that far, check Section 6 first.

You may also find what you are looking for on the group website.
